# Evidence Framework

## Source Priority

1. Direct IBD vs IBS stool or fecal comparisons
2. Targeted or replicated fecal validation of the same molecule
3. Stool handling or stability studies for the molecule
4. Primary bacterial sensing or regulator papers with ligand-level evidence
5. Reviews only for lead generation, followed by primary-source confirmation

## Search Patterns

Use targeted searches instead of broad disease reviews.

- Stage 1:
  - `IBD IBS stool metabolomics`
  - `fecal metabolomics Crohn's ulcerative colitis irritable bowel syndrome`
  - `<molecule> IBD IBS stool`
- Stage 2:
  - `<molecule> stool stability`
  - `<molecule> feces stability freeze thaw`
  - `<molecule> anaerobic degradation gut`
- Stage 3:
  - `<molecule> E. coli sensor`
  - `<molecule> Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1 regulator`
  - `<molecule> bacterial transcriptional regulator`

## Confidence Guide

- `5`: Direct IBD vs IBS stool evidence or direct bacterial ligand-response evidence with gene-level support
- `4`: Strong stool evidence with replication, but indirect disease comparator or incomplete mechanistic support
- `3`: Single-cohort stool evidence or stability or sensing evidence inferred from a close context
- `2`: Only non-stool or non-comparative support
- `1`: Speculative lead with no reliable engineering basis

## Red Flags

- Pathway labels used as if they were molecules
- Untargeted features without confident analyte identification
- Tissue-only or serum-only signals presented as stool biomarkers
- Host receptors or immune pathways presented as bacterial sensors
- Claimed sensing systems without a named regulator, gene, promoter, or direct bacterial response experiment
